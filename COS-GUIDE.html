C COMPANY · 40 SAR · COUGAR

COS Handover Guide

Duty procedures and the Cougar System: report sick, book out / book in, conducts, and the parade state.
For the incoming Company Orderly Sergeant. Read the whole guide once before your first duty. After that, use Section 9 (Quick Reference) day to day. If the app cannot handle something, contact Youqi.
THE PRIME DIRECTIVE

The parade state from the app must be 100% accurate at all times. Not just at first parade. At all times. Log every move in or out of camp the moment it happens. Log every new status the moment you learn of it. Do not save entries up for later.

There is one exception. A very small error that will last for exactly one parade state may be fixed by hand in the report text before you copy it. Nothing else. If you cannot log a situation correctly, or you are not sure how, contact Youqi at once. Do not guess. Do not leave it wrong.

Everything in this guide serves that rule. The app builds strength, ATTC, and the out-of-camp list from what you log. Log well and the parade state is always right. Log late and every report you send is a guess.

2Report sick: the full loop

This is your most common and most important task. It has app steps and physical steps. Steps marked PHYSICAL happen outside the app. They are just as mandatory.

Why Pending matters

Pending entries show in the REPORT SICK section of the parade state on the day you log them. That is how the company knows who is at the MO. When the outcome comes back, you upgrade the same entry (Section 2.3). Never log a second one.

2.2 The report sick list to Dr. Nigel

Send the list in this format and no other. The header line is DDMMYY C COY 40SAR HHMMH. Split cases into URTI and NON-URTI. URTI means upper respiratory symptoms: sore throat, cough, runny nose, with or without fever. Everything else is NON-URTI, including fever alone. Zero-pad counts and serial numbers to two digits. Serial numbers restart at 01 in each section. Each entry has S/N, R/N (rank, name, 4D), and REASON, with temperature first if taken.

2.3 When he comes back from the MO

  1. PHYSICAL Ask him to send you (the COS) a photo of his MC / status chit. Check what he tells you against the document. The document wins.
  2. Press the ✎ edit button on his row in the Medical tab and change Pending to the actual outcome.
    🏥 Medical→ his row→ ✎→ Status: Pending → actual
    Change the Status dropdown from Pending to the real status. Fill in Start and End, both inclusive, straight off the chit. The main statuses:
    • MC / Warded: away from camp. Not counted in strength. Listed under ATTC.
    • LD: in camp, restricted. Listed under MEDICAL STATUS.
    • Excuse ... (Heavy Load, Kneeling, Squatting, Uniform, RMJ, Swimming, Prolonged Standing, Upper Limb, Lower Limb): in camp. Listed under MEDICAL STATUS.
    • NIL: MO cleared him with nothing. Leave the dates blank. The entry closes on its own.
    If he got more than one status (for example 2D LD + 4D Excuse RMJ), press ＋ Add another status in the same form. One recruit, one entry, many statuses.
    If he got an MC but stays in camp to consume it, tick Consume in camp. He then counts in strength and shows under MEDICAL STATUS instead of ATTC.
  3. PHYSICAL Have him sign out of the report sick book.
  4. PHYSICAL If his status needs a yellow band, draw one and sign it out in the status band book. When the status ends, collect the band and sign it back in. The app does not track bands. The band book is the only record. Keep it as tight as the app.

3Book out and book in

Anyone who leaves camp for any reason other than an active MC or Warded status must be booked out. The record must also end at the right time. One form handles it all: Book Out of Camp.

🚪 Book out for todayCancel

Today only is for same-day moves: sent to a hospital, an appointment, an errand. It clears at midnight. If the person is still away the next morning, he needs a new record, or should have had a date range from the start.

Date range (leave) is for anything longer. Pick a Type: Off-in-Lieu, Annual Leave, Compassionate Leave, Weekend, Night's Out, Course, Guard Duty, NDP, Other. Start and End are inclusive. The person counts as out for every day in the range. He counts back in from the day after End.

3.2 The four levers on a person's row

ButtonWhen you press it

To change the dates of a leave record (early or late book-in), go to 📅 Out / Leave and press ✎ on the record. This opens Edit Leave/Out Entry. Adjust End and Days so the app books him back in on the right day.

MO sends a recruit out to CGH, with an escort commander
  1. Book out the recruit, Today only. Roster → his row → 🚪 Book out → Today only → Reason: Sent out to CGH from MO. Strength is now correct while he is at the hospital.
  2. Book out the escort commander the same way. Reason: CGH escort for C1203. Commanders count in strength too. Miss the escort and the COMMANDERS line is wrong.
  3. When the outcome comes back, update the recruit's medical entry. Medical tab → ✎ his Pending row → set Warded or MC with the real dates. From tomorrow, the medical status covers his absence in ATTC. The today-only book-out expires at midnight. No gap, no double count.
  4. Update the commander's book-in timing. If he returns tonight, press ↩ Book in on his row as soon as he is back. Any report after that shows him in camp. If he stays overnight with the recruit, book him out again in the morning, or log a Date range that covers the stay.

After you press Book:

  • The appointment shows on the Dashboard under 📅 Upcoming Appointments, and in every parade state under MEDICAL APPT, until you resolve it.
  • On the day, the row gets a TODAY badge and a one-tap 🚪 Out button. Press it when he leaves camp. It books him out with reason Appt: <reason>. In the parade state he moves to OTHERS as <reason> (MA), with Location, Date, and Time.
  • When he returns, press ↩ Book in on the same row.
  • After the appointment is over, press ✓ on the row. That marks it resolved and removes it from the dashboard and the parade state.
  • If the appointment gave him a new status, log it in the Medical tab (Section 2.3).

The FP / LP modal also shows a checklist: 📅 Outside appointments today — tick = booked OUT of camp. Use it to confirm who is really out at parade time.

Knee specialist appointment, start to finish

On 16/05, Dalson's section commander receives an appointment slip: knee specialist review at CGH, 22/05 at 0930H. He informs the COS the same day. The COS logs it at once: Dashboard → + Book, with Out of camp ticked. From 16/05 to 22/05, every parade state lists the appointment under MEDICAL APPT. No one is surprised on the day. On 22/05 at 0830H, Dalson leaves camp. The COS presses 🚪 Out on the appointment row. The morning parade state shows him under OTHERS as Knee specialist review (MA). He returns at 1400H. The COS presses ↩ Book in. The specialist gave him 2 weeks of Excuse Lower Limb, so the COS logs it in the Medical tab (Section 2.3). Last, the COS presses ✓ on the appointment row to resolve it.

Weekend: BMT stays in, PTP books out
  1. Book out PTP as a block. 📅 Out / Leave → + Log → Apply to: the PTP program → Date range (leave) → Type Weekend → Start Saturday, End Sunday. The app shows the head-count and skips anyone already out (on MC, on leave). Read that message. Make sure the number matches reality before you press 📅 Log leave / out.
  2. Do nothing for BMT. Never log people who stay in camp. Log movement only.
  3. Book-in timing. With End = Sunday, everyone counts back in from Monday 0000H. Monday's First Parade State is then correct on its own. At Sunday-night book-in, check faces against the 📅 Out / Leave list. If someone did not book in, extend his record (✎ → push End out) and chase him. The app must never say he is in camp when he is not.
  4. Exceptions in the block. A PTP recruit staying in for the weekend: press ✓ Book in anyway on him each day he is in, or ✎ his own leave record. A recruit back early on Sunday morning: ✎ his record, End = Saturday.
Night's out

The company gets an evening out and is back by 2200H the same day. Use Date range (leave) with Type Night's Out. That type means same day, evening off camp. Scope: Whole company or the platoons going. Check everyone at book-in. The records close on their own after the day ends.

Booking out people who are already out

Bulk book-outs skip people who are already out on MC or leave, and tell you how many they skipped. If you book out one person who is already out, the app blocks you and says so. Never add a second record "to be safe". One person, one reason for being out. Double records break the strength count.

5Groups

Groups are ad-hoc squads that cut across platoons: guard duty, details, rehearsal parties. Use them so bulk actions hit exactly the right people.

👥 Roster→ ⦿ Groups→ New group: "Guard Duty"→ Create + pick members
  • ⦿ Groups: name it, pick members, done. ✎ Members edits the list later. Deleting a group does not touch the members.
  • ▣ Combined groups mix platoons, programs, and groups with + and −. Tap a chip once to include (+). Tap again to exclude (−). Example: tap P4, then tap ⦿ Guard Duty twice. You get P4 − Guard Duty: all of Platoon 4 except guard duty.
  • Groups show up as scopes in the Book Out form and the conduct wizard, and as a filter in the top bar.
Guard duty draw

Eight recruits from three platoons go on guard duty at another camp, out Friday to Saturday. Create group Guard Duty with those eight. Book Out form → Apply to ⦿ Guard Duty (8) → Date range → Type Guard Duty → Fri to Sat. For Saturday's PT, log the conduct with a scope like ▣ P4 − Guard Duty where needed. Total Str is then right without hand-counting.

  • Is OTHERS exactly the set of people who booked out?

You can edit the text before copying. That is for the one-parade-state exception only: a typo in a reason, or a movement from 5 minutes ago that resolves within the hour. If you would need the same hand-edit twice, fix the data instead. If you cannot fix it in the data, contact Youqi.

The same menu gives you the 🏥 Medical Status List and 🦵 MSK Report when asked.

7How the app counts strength (know this cold)

A person is out of camp today only if one of these three is true:

  1. An active MC or Warded status covers today, and Consume in camp is not ticked. → shown in ATTC.
  2. An active leave / out record covers today (Start ≤ today ≤ End). → shown in OTHERS.
  3. A same-day book-out was pressed today. → shown in OTHERS. Clears at midnight.

CURRENT STRENGTH = TOTAL STRENGTH minus everyone above. So every wrong parade state is one missing or stale record in one of those three places. When a number looks off, check Medical, then Out / Leave, then today's book-outs, in that order.

8Sync discipline

  • Edits save locally first. Watch the ⟳ sync status at the top right. Where a tab offers Push to Sheet after edits, use it.
  • Do not generate a parade state while another device still holds unsynced edits. One device: log, sync, then generate.
  • If sync looks stuck, or two devices disagree, stop editing and contact Youqi. Do not fix a sync conflict by typing entries again. That creates duplicates.

HANDOVER STANDARD

You hand over duty with the app matching reality exactly. The report sick book and band book match the Medical tab. No unlogged movements. The next COS should be able to generate a parade state in their first minute of duty and send it without checking anything. That is the standard, because that is what you received.
