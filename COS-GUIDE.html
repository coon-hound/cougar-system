C COMPANY · 40 SAR · COUGAR

COS Handover Guide

Duty procedures and the Cougar System: report sick, book out / book in, conducts, and the parade state.
For the incoming Company Orderly Sergeant. Read this once fully before your first duty, then use Section 9 (Quick Reference) day to day. App escalation point of contact: Youqi.
THE PRIME DIRECTIVE

The parade state generated by the app must be 100% accurate at all times. Not at first parade, not when someone asks for it. At all times. Every person who steps in or out of camp, and every status a recruit gains or loses, is logged in the app at the moment it happens, not batched for later.

The only tolerated exception: a very minor discrepancy that will exist for exactly one parade state. For that single report you may hand-edit the generated text before copying it out. If a situation cannot be represented accurately in the app, or you are unsure how to log something, contact Youqi immediately. Do not improvise, and do not leave it wrong.

Everything in this guide serves that directive. The app derives strength, the ATTC list, and the out-of-camp list from what you log. If you log faithfully, the parade state writes itself and is always right. If you log lazily, every report you send is a guess.

2Report sick: the full loop

This is your most frequent and most important workflow. It has app steps and physical steps. Steps marked PHYSICAL happen outside the app and are equally mandatory.

Why Pending matters

Pending entries appear in the REPORT SICK section of the parade state on the day they are logged. That is how the company knows who is at the MO. When the outcome comes back you upgrade the same entry (Section 2.3); you never log a second one.

2.2 The report sick list to Dr. Nigel

Send the consolidated list in this format, and only this format. Header line is DDMMYY C COY 40SAR HHMMH. Cases are split into URTI (upper respiratory symptoms: sore throat, cough, runny nose, with or without fever) and NON-URTI (everything else, including fever or high temperature without respiratory symptoms). Counts and serial numbers are zero-padded to two digits, and serial numbers restart at 01 in each section. Each entry is S/N, R/N (rank, name, 4D), REASON (temperature first if taken).

2.3 When he comes back from the MO

  1. PHYSICAL Ask him to send you (the COS) a photo of his MC / status chit. Cross-check what he tells you against the actual document. The document wins.
  2. Press the ✎ edit button on his row in the Medical tab and upgrade Pending to the actual outcome.
    🏥 Medical→ his row→ ✎→ Status: Pending → actual
    Change the Status dropdown from Pending to the real status and fill in Start and End (both inclusive, straight off the chit). The main statuses:
    • MC / Warded: away from camp. Counted out of strength and listed under ATTC.
    • LD: in camp, restricted. Listed under MEDICAL STATUS.
    • Excuse ... (Heavy Load, Kneeling, Squatting, Uniform, RMJ, Swimming, Prolonged Standing, Upper Limb, Lower Limb): in camp, listed under MEDICAL STATUS.
    • NIL: MO cleared him with nothing. Dates stay blank; the entry closes out cleanly.
    If he got multiple statuses (for example 2D LD + 4D Excuse RMJ), press ＋ Add another status inside the same form. One recruit, one entry, multiple statuses.
    If he got an MC but is consuming it in camp (staying in), tick Consume in camp. He then stays counted in strength and appears under MEDICAL STATUS instead of ATTC.
  3. PHYSICAL Have him sign out of the report sick book.
  4. PHYSICAL If his status warrants a yellow band, draw one and sign it out in the status band book. When the status ends, collect the band back and sign it in. The app does not track bands; the band book is the single record. Keep it as tight as the app.

Recruit reports sick outside camp during his weekend

REC Amos messages you Saturday night: he saw a GP and got 2 days MC. Log it now, not Monday. 🏥 Medical → + Log Report Sick, fill Location with the clinic name (that is what the field is for), status MC with the dates from his MC photo. Sunday's and Monday's parade states are now already correct, and he appears under ATTC automatically. When he books in, the report sick book and band book steps happen then.

3Book out and book in

Anyone physically leaving camp for any reason other than an active MC/Warded status must be booked out, and the record must end at the right time. One flow handles everything: the Book Out of Camp form.

🚪 Book out for todayCancel

Today only is for same-day movements: sent out to a hospital, an appointment, an errand. It clears itself at midnight, so a person still away the next morning needs a fresh record (or should have been a date range in the first place).

Date range (leave) is for anything longer, with a Type: Off-in-Lieu, Annual Leave, Compassionate Leave, Weekend, Night's Out, Course, Guard Duty, NDP, Other. Start and End are inclusive: the person is counted out of camp for the whole of every day in the range, and back in strength from the day after End.

3.2 The four levers on a person's row

ButtonWhen you press it
🚪 Book outHe is stepping out of camp. Opens the form above.
↩ Book inHe came back from a same-day book-out before midnight. Reverses today's book-out so he counts present again now.
✓ Book in anywayHe is on MC or leave in the app but is physically in camp today. Counts him present for today only (shows In camp · manual).
✕ Undo book-inYou pressed one of the book-in buttons by mistake.

To change the dates of an existing leave record (early book-in, extended leave), go to 📅 Out / Leave and press ✎ on the record to open Edit Leave/Out Entry. Adjust End (and Days) so the app flips him back into strength on the correct day.

MO sends a recruit out to CGH, with an escort commander
  1. Book out the recruit, Today only. Roster → his row → 🚪 Book out → Today only → Reason: Sent out to CGH from MO. Strength is now correct while he is at the hospital.
  2. Book out the escort commander the same way. Reason: CGH escort for C1203. Commanders count toward strength too; an unbooked escort makes the COMMANDERS line wrong.
  3. When the recruit's outcome comes back, update his medical entry. Medical tab → ✎ his Pending row → set Warded or MC with the actual dates. From tomorrow the medical status carries his absence in ATTC; the today-only book-out expires by itself at midnight, so there is no gap and no double-count.
  4. Update the commander's book-in timing. If he returns tonight, press ↩ Book in on his row the moment he is back so any report generated after that shows him in camp. If he stays overnight with the recruit, book him out again tomorrow morning (or log a Date range covering the stay).
Recruit books out for a medical appointment

On the day of the appointment: Roster → his row → 🚪 Book out. If the appointment is already recorded in the app, the form shows 📅 Outside appointment today and prefills the reason as Appt: <reason>. Keep Today only and submit. He appears in the parade state under OTHERS with an (MA) marker. When he books back in, press ↩ Book in. If the appointment produced a new status (an MC, a new excuse), log it in the Medical tab exactly as in Section 2.3.

Weekend: BMT stays in, PTP books out
  1. Book out PTP as a block. 📅 Out / Leave → + Log → Apply to: the PTP program → Date range (leave) → Type Weekend → Start Saturday, End Sunday. The app confirms the head-count and skips anyone already out (on MC, on leave); read that confirmation and make sure the number matches reality before pressing 📅 Log leave / out.
  2. Do nothing for BMT. People staying in camp are never logged. Only movement is logged.
  3. Book-in timing. With End = Sunday, everyone counts back in strength from Monday 0000H, so Monday's First Parade State is automatically correct. During Sunday-night book-in, verify faces against the 📅 Out / Leave list. Anyone who fails to book in gets his record extended (✎ → push End date out) and gets chased; do not let the app say he is in camp when he is not.
  4. Exceptions inside the block. A PTP recruit staying in for the weekend: after the bulk book-out, press ✓ Book in anyway on him for the days he is in, or ✎ his individual leave record. A recruit returning early Sunday morning: ✎ his record, End = Saturday.
Night's out

The company gets an evening out, back by 2200H the same day. Use Date range (leave) with Type Night's Out (it is the same-day, evening-off-camp type), scope Whole company or the platoons going. At book-in, verify everyone is back; the records close themselves after the day ends.

Booking out people who are already out

Bulk book-outs automatically skip people already out via MC or leave, and tell you how many were skipped. If you try to book out one person who is already out, the app blocks you and says so. Never stack a second record on someone to "make sure": one person, one reason for being out. Double records are how strength counts go wrong.

5Groups

Groups are ad-hoc squads that cut across platoons (guard duty, details, rehearsal parties). Use them so bulk actions hit exactly the right people.

👥 Roster→ ⦿ Groups→ New group: "Guard Duty"→ Create + pick members
  • ⦿ Groups: name it, pick members, done. ✎ Members edits membership later. Deleting a group does not touch the members.
  • ▣ Combined groups mix platoons, programs, and groups with + and −. Tap a chip once to include (+), twice to exclude (−). Example: tap P4, then tap ⦿ Guard Duty twice, and you get P4 − Guard Duty: all of Platoon 4 except the guard duty personnel.
  • Groups appear as scopes in the Book Out form, in the conduct wizard, and as a filter in the top bar.
Guard duty draw

Eight recruits from three platoons are drawn for guard duty at another camp, out Friday to Saturday. Create group Guard Duty with those eight. Book Out form → Apply to ⦿ Guard Duty (8) → Date range → Type Guard Duty → Fri to Sat. For Saturday's PT you log the conduct with scope ▣ P4 − Guard Duty style combined groups where needed, so Total Str is right without hand-counting.

  • Does CURRENT STRENGTH match your physical count? If not, someone's movement was not logged. Find it and log it; do not massage the number.
  • Is everyone in ATTC actually away, and everyone in REPORT SICK actually at the MO?
  • Is OTHERS exactly the set of people who booked out?

The textarea is editable before copying. That is for the single-parade-state exception only (a typo in a reason, a movement that happened 5 minutes ago and resolves within the hour). Anything that would need the same hand-edit twice must be fixed in the data, and anything you cannot fix in the data goes to Youqi.

The same menu gives you the 🏥 Medical Status List and 🦵 MSK Report when asked for them.

7How the app counts strength (know this cold)

A person is out of camp today if and only if one of these three is true:

  1. An active MC or Warded status covers today (and is not marked Consume in camp). → shown in ATTC.
  2. An active leave / out record covers today (Start ≤ today ≤ End). → shown in OTHERS.
  3. A same-day book-out was pressed today. → shown in OTHERS; clears at midnight.

CURRENT STRENGTH = TOTAL STRENGTH minus everyone matching the above. Therefore: every wrong parade state is one missing or one stale record in exactly one of those three places. When a number looks off, check Medical, then Out / Leave, then today's book-outs, in that order.

8Sync discipline

  • Edits save locally first. Watch the ⟳ sync status at the top right; where a tab offers a Push to Sheet action after edits, use it.
  • Do not generate a parade state while your last edits are still unsynced on another device. One device, log, sync, then generate.
  • If sync looks stuck or two devices disagree, stop editing and contact Youqi. Do not "fix" a sync conflict by re-typing entries; that creates duplicates.

HANDOVER STANDARD

You hand over duty with: the app matching physical reality exactly, the report sick book and band book reconciled against the Medical tab, and zero unlogged movements. The incoming COS should be able to generate a parade state in their first minute of duty and send it without checking anything. That is the standard, because that is what you received.
