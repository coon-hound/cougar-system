🚶 Cougar System — Dev Playground

Loads a demo company into this browser (no backend, no invite) so you can play with the Movement Board.

  • 40 recruits across 4 platoons (PTP: Plt 1+4 · BMT: Plt 2+3) + 3 commanders.
  • Some already split across Range / Cookhouse / Lecture Hall for today; the rest in Main Body.
  • 6 out of camp (MC · Warded · leave) so the read-only "Out of Camp" card is populated.
